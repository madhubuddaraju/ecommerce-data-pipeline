## Key findings

1. **Growth is strong and seasonal.** Revenue grew 291% year over year, and November-December
   alone account for 36% of all revenue. Inventory and marketing budgets should be front-loaded into Q4.
2. **Retention decays steadily after the first purchase.** On average 53% of a cohort
   buys again in month 1, falling to 35% by month 6. A post-purchase onboarding flow
   (days 7-30) is the biggest retention lever.
3. **Champions carry the business.** 927 Champions (26% of buyers)
   generate 66% of revenue. A loyalty tier would protect this base.
4. **216 high-value customers are At Risk**
   (avg 101 days since last order), holding $681.2K of historical spend -
   the best target for a win-back campaign.
5. **Channel quality varies 2.6x.** A
   referral customer is worth $3,683 vs
   $1,409 for social; budget should shift toward
   referral and away from low-converting social spend.
6. **Electronics leads revenue** ($4.55M, 44% margin).
